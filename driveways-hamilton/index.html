About Driveways in Hamilton, and What Do the Experts Do?

Driveways are made by pouring concrete or asphalt on a prepared bed of subgrade. The subgrade is the layer of compacted earth beneath your driveway. The experts at Hamilton Concrete and Paving recommend preparing the subgrade by removing any grass, vines, or other growth.

Our concrete Hamilton specialists also recommend compacting the soil to create a solid foundation. Once the subgrade is prepared, the concrete or asphalt can be poured. The thickness of the concrete or asphalt will depend on the amount of traffic the driveway will need to support. 

For example, driveways that will be used by heavy vehicles will need to be thicker than those that will only be used by passenger vehicles. After the concrete or asphalt has been poured, it will need to be allowed to cure before it can be used. Curing times vary depending on the temperature and humidity, but they typically range from 24 to 48 hours.

Once the driveway has cured, it should be sealed to protect it from weathering and wear. Sealing should be done every one to three years, depending on the condition of the driveway and the amount of traffic it receives. By following these steps, construction experts ensure that your driveway will be durable and long-lasting.
